# Chapter 4 — Chemical bonds

## Ionic bonding
Ionic bonding is electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions. Electron transfer can create these ions, but attraction is the bond itself.

## Covalent bonding
A covalent bond involves a shared pair of electrons between atoms. Sharing and transfer are distinct descriptions; neither should be substituted for the other.

## Bond polarity
Unequal attraction for shared electrons creates a polar bond. Molecular shape also matters when determining whether the entire molecule is polar.

## Metallic bonding
Metallic bonding involves attraction between positive metal cores and delocalized electrons. Mobile electrons help explain electrical conductivity.

## Structure and properties
Material properties depend on structure and bonding. A change in bonding description must be justified using the actual particle arrangement, not only the material's name.
